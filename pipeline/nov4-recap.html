Upgrades to the model

New rules from last night

Step one rung below the average

When a line sits right on a player's average, take the next rung down or pass.

7 legs missed by 1, including KD 21 on 22.5, which cost the $50 bonus.

No rebound unders when frontcourt injuries add minutes

If injuries give a player more minutes, his rebounds go up. Flagg (Lively out) and Ighodaro (Fleming out) both cleared their lines.

Rebound unders went 2 for 6.

A flagged risk gets cut

If I write down the reason a leg could fail, it doesn't go on a big ticket. I flagged Ighodaro's minutes and Flagg's rebounds, then kept both anyway.

Ighodaro alone sank two Kalshi tickets.

No rebound overs on shooters just because a big is out
