HOW WE GRADE NOW
UPDATED NOV 20 · ENGINE V8

Current record, Nov 4 to Nov 19: 755-432 on 1187 graded legs (64%). Graded 70+: 345-103 (77%). These are the cappers' picks, not every line on the board.

ENGINE V8 (FROM THE NOV 20 LATE GAMES)
Four AI analysts grade every leg blind: no Kalshi or sportsbook price is shown, so they have to do the research (minutes and role, injuries and news, the matchup and primary defender, shot zones, rest and travel, pace, and weather for football). Our grade is the middle of their four numbers plus our own system grade. Then Kalshi referees: at or above us is GREEN, 1 to 7 below us is YELLOW, and 8 or more below us is pulled, because that gap has lost most often in our record. Each agent also runs 1,000 simulations per game on near-even lines, shown on Research, Beta Test Mode.
FROM NOV 20
ENGINE V7 (NOV 20 EARLY GAMES)
Four AI models grade every leg, starting from the Kalshi price and moving off it only for a named reason. We publish the middle of their numbers, capped at Kalshi + 7. If that middle number is 8 or more above Kalshi, the leg is removed from the board, the Hammer and every ticket, and shown on Research as "market disagrees". Legs with no Kalshi price max out at 75 and never go on the Hammer. The board shows GREEN legs (Kalshi at or above us) and YELLOW legs (1 to 7 above) graded 70+. Our own system grade is still computed and saved next to the models' numbers.
FROM NOV 20
HAMMER UNDER V7
3 to 5 legs from different games, each published 75+ with Kalshi at or above us or within 3, points, assists, threes or favorite moneylines, no back-to-backs, paying 2x or more. If fewer than 3 legs qualify, there is no Hammer that night. Passing is a win.
FROM NOV 20
THROUGH NOV 19, GRADES CAME FROM OUR ORIGINAL PROCESS
Each pick is graded from the player's season hit rate on that line, his last 5 games, the matchup and back-to-backs, then capped. Team lines are graded from the sportsbook spread. The Kalshi price is shown next to every leg but never changes the grade.
CURRENT
⚠ LEGS STAY OFF HAMMERS AND MAIN TICKETS
A ⚠ means our grade is 8 or more points above Kalshi. Those legs hit less often than the rest on the Nov 4 to 17 record, including nights that were not used to set the rule. Those 37 legs came from 20 games, so this is still a small sample. Nov 19 added 30 more like-for-like legs: ⚠ legs hit 2 of 8, 0 to 7 above Kalshi 0 of 4, below Kalshi 7 of 18. Kalshi's prices were closer to the results than our grades again (accuracy score 0.279 vs 0.313, lower is better).
