# BettorDays Product Audit · [DATE]

You are a product team auditing a sports betting research app: a founder, a senior product designer, a growth lead, a sharp sports bettor, and a compliance lead from a licensed sportsbook. Be blunt. Praise is useless to us.

**The app:** https://bettordays.xyz (open it and click through every tab: Picks, Ticket Builder, Research, History, Results, How it works, Pick & Post, Chat, Leaders). It is free right now. Every pick gets a hit-probability grade before tip-off, shows the Kalshi price next to our grade, and every result is graded the next morning in a public record. Four AI agents grade each leg blind and run 1,000-game simulations. There is a Master filter for the groups that have hit most.

**The founder's goal:** build a billion-dollar company that actually puts money in bettors' pockets. The picks have to be that good, and the app has to be that easy: a new user should find tonight's best legs and build a smart ticket in under 30 seconds.

**Our record so far (Nov 4 to 19):** 1,195 graded legs. Legs graded 70+ with Kalshi at or above our grade went 124-17 (88%). Legs where we were 8+ above Kalshi went 55-45. NBA points, assists, threes and favorite moneylines hit best. PRA, NFL yardage, touchdowns and college lines hit worst.

## Do this

1. Use the site like a first-time bettor on a phone. Where did you get lost, bored or confused? Quote the exact screen and words.
2. Use it like a sharp bettor. What is missing that would make you pay $30 a month?
3. Ideas the founder is considering. Judge each one (do it, change it, or drop it), and say why:
   - Parlay grader: upload a screenshot of any slip and get each leg graded, the weakest leg flagged and a safer swap suggested
   - Sign-up bonus finder by state, with a referral code
   - Referral program (what to give the referrer and the friend)
   - Founders pricing and a paid tier (what is free, what is paid)
4. Growth: the fastest honest way to the first 1,000 active users and the first $10,000 a month.
5. Compliance: what could get this shut down or sued (affiliate licensing by state, 21+ rules, responsible gambling, advertising claims, Apple and Google store rules). Be specific.
6. Trust: what would make a skeptical bettor believe the record is real.
