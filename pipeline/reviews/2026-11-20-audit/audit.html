The number that matters

Hit rate is not profit

Grok and Gemini both hit the same point: an 88% leg bought at 88 cents makes nothing. So we priced every graded 70+ leg from Nov 4 to 19 as a single $1 Kalshi buy at the price we logged, with Kalshi's fee taken out.

+6.5%GREEN singles, 141 legs
−10.2%GREEN singles last week only
−18.1%Legs we graded 8+ above Kalshi

GREEN made money overall, and the REMOVED rule saved us. But look at GREEN week by week. Almost all of the profit came from one hot week (Nov 9 to 15, 73 wins and 2 losses). The last week lost money.

By stat type, the Master filter groups (threes, NBA moneylines, assists) are the only ones in the green. PRA and rebounds lose money even at 70+.

Small samples. Threes is 23 legs and PRA is 16, so read these as direction, not law. Parlays lose more than singles because Kalshi pays less than fair on combos.

Consensus

Where all four agree

  • Take "FREE MONEY" off /bonuses and "making people money" off the popup

    ALL 4

    The lawyer seat in every report called these the lines a regulator or the FTC quotes. Add 1-800-GAMBLER to every page and post, and drop the old 8 to 10% bankroll line.

  • Stop paying models to browse. Feed them one fact pack per game

    ALL 4

    Injury report, last 10 games, minutes, spread, price. Browsing is what made the $9 day. With a fixed pack the cost drops below $1 a day and the models become comparable.

  • Run simulations in code, not in a chat model

    ALL 4

    The AI sims disagreed wildly (Brooks 2+ threes: Claude 83, ChatGPT 54). Our own code sim already exists. It costs $0 and gives the same answer every time.

  • The first phone screen should be tonight's play, with one Copy button

    ALL 4

    Home is 26 phone screens and History is 275. Show the Hammer (or "we passed") and last night's result, and put the rest one tap away.

  • Parlay Grader is the best lead magnet. Make it a main tab

    ALL 4

    It's the only one-screen page. Use cheap vision to read the slip, look the legs up in our graded board, and circle the leg that kills it.

  • No paid tier yet

    ALL 4

    Wait until a logged record beats the closing price. A paid pick is the claim a regulator reads first.

  • Pause the referral push and keep bonus links unpaid

    ALL 4

    At 23 members, referrals have nothing to spread yet. Paid sportsbook links need a state license in most states.

  • Cappers: ask them first, X API as backup, never scrape

    ALL 4

    Grok: ask for a fixed-format drop. Gemini: the official X API (it claims about $9 to $15 a month for 20 cappers, not yet verified).

Disagreements

Where they split

Who referees the price

Gemini: drop Kalshi as the referee and pay for sharp sportsbook odds (Pinnacle, FanDuel, DraftKings) at about $30 to $50 a month. Grok: keep it simple, report ROI and closing-line value first.

How to make money

Gemini: a $29 Pro tier plus pay-per-signup affiliate links in states with no license fee, for about $10,300 a month at scale. Grok: $0 until 60 logged nights beat the close, and paid links are a licensing problem.

Which models to trust

Gemini: Claude was the outlier on Nov 20, so stop being Claude-only. Grok: four models reading the same news is one opinion four times. No weights until each has 150 graded legs.

Bottom nav

Gemini: 4 tabs (Board, Grader, Proof, Me). Grok and Claude: 5 tabs (Tonight, Ticket, Grade, Record, More).
