      How easy is three? the model, explained for a 10-year-old

      Picture a jar of 100 marbles. 82 are green, 18 are red. That's our 70%+ legs: 68 of 83 hit since Nov 4. A parlay is reaching in and pulling marbles. You only cash if every marble is green.

      Tap the button to pull three marbles from the jar at random.

      Hitting is not the same as profit
      price matters

      Three green marbles happens about 55 times in 100. But Kalshi prices a 3-leg like that around 1.6x to 1.8x, so you win about $0.70 when it hits and lose $1 when it misses. Over time that's close to break-even. The edge comes from legs the market prices worse than we grade them, and from passing on bad prices.

      Grades are our estimates, not promises. 21+, bet what you can lose.

          The algorithm · run this on every capper board

          1. Pool. Only legs I grade 70%+. NBA and NFL. No college football, no UFC.
          2. Filter. Cut any leg that hits a flag: backup or new QB on passing and receiving legs, guard rebound overs, assist overs, PRA combos, back-to-backs, players new to a team, James Harden.
          3. Hook check. If the line sits within one of the player's average, drop one rung.
          4. Spread it out. Separate games. Two legs from one game only when they share a script, like a favorite's ML plus its lead back.
          5. Build the ladder. Core 8 (about 1 in 3), Ladder 12 (about 1 in 5), and the 20 only on slates with 20 clean legs. Never pad with 60s to reach 20.
          6. Spice, max two. One star ceiling line in a good script and one top TD pick. Each roughly doubles the payout. Top 4 TDs went 3 of 4 Sunday.
          7. Shop the price. Kalshi paid 10 to 30% of fair on long combos. Price the 12 and the 20 somewhere else before locking.
          8. Size it. $10 to $25 a lotto. The hammer stays the main bet.
